About

A bit about me

I'm a second-year MS Robotics Engineering student at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, graduating May 2027. My research focus is autonomous UGV perception -- teaching ground robots to understand what terrain they're driving on and how to navigate it safely.

At WPI's Autonomous Vehicle Mobility Institute (AVMI), I work on terrain segmentation using deep learning (SegFormer) and build LiDAR-camera fusion pipelines in ROS2. I'm also a Graduate Teaching Assistant for Control Engineering and Differential Equations.

Before grad school, I worked as a Robotics Competition trainer in India, mentoring FRC, FTC, and WRO teams. That experience gave me a foundation in hands-on system building and a knack for explaining complex things simply.
